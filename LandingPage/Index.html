About

Smarter Veterinary Care.
Connected Clinics. Healthier Communities.

VetIntel is a web-based veterinary intelligence platform that helps veterinary clinics manage their daily operations while transforming anonymized health data into meaningful veterinary insights.

What makes VetIntel different?

Unlike traditional veterinary management systems that focus mainly on individual clinic operations, VetIntel connects participating clinics through anonymized and aggregated health information. This helps identify disease trends, monitor health risks, and provide community-level veterinary intelligence while protecting private owner information.

Privacy

Your Data. Your Privacy. Our Responsibility.

VetIntel separates private clinic records from shared veterinary intelligence. Personally identifiable owner information is not included in the shared intelligence layer. Only appropriate anonymized and aggregated indicators are used for analytics and disease surveillance.

Private Clinic Data

Owner information, pet identity, medical records, and clinic-specific information remain protected within the clinic environment.

Anonymized Intelligence

Disease categories, risk indicators, vaccination patterns, aggregated counts, and trends may be used for veterinary intelligence without exposing individual owner identities.
