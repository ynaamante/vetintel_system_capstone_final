# VetIntel Admin Design System

- Product: veterinary-clinic administration workspace.
- Typeface: DM Sans, with 400–700 weights.
- Primary colors: deep forest `#013826`, botanical teal `#086b50`, pale sage `#e7f4ec`, warm ivory `#fafbf8`, white `#ffffff`.
- Supporting colors: ink `#172b24`, muted text `#5a6661`, border `#bed6c5`, success `#16a34a`, warning `#d97706`, error `#d6453d`.
- Surfaces: white cards over warm ivory or very pale sage backgrounds; cards have fine green-gray borders, restrained shadows, and 10–14px radius.
- Shared shell: forest sidebar at left; full-width green-gradient topbar with page title/subtitle, notification control, and signed-in user account control.
- Detail workspaces: a topbar with a Back control, a horizontal pale-sage summary card, structured information panels, an expandable permissions workspace, and a plain footer with a secondary Close action.
- Responsive behavior: preserve content hierarchy; summary cards wrap, two-column content stacks to one column, controls remain touch-sized, and tables/permission views scroll rather than truncate.
