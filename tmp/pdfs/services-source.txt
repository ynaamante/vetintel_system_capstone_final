Billing notes: A vaccination service may include the vaccine dose; avoid charging it twice. A booster is a dose event, not a | 
separate vaccine inventory product. Surgery and hospitalization often require additional itemized charges. Labels such as | 
5-in-1, 6-in-1, and 8-in-1 must be linked to the actual vaccine product and antigen coverage. |
